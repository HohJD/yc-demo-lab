# YC RFS Inventory

Unofficial. Scraped from the official YC Requests for Startups pages (https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs) across 9 editions — 107 requests in total. Every request has a route at `/demos/<slug>`.

Levels: **Real prototype** = genuine logic runs on your input; **Simulation** = interactive model with illustrative data; **Concept demo** = scripted interactive workflow; **Planned** = not built.
